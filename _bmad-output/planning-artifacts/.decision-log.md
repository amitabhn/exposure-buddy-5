# PRD Decision Log — Exposure Buddy

## 2026-06-07 — Market scope reframe, cost objective removal, MVP/Phase 1 split

### Decision 1: India reframed as launch market, not primary market
**Signal:** Product owner direction.
**Change:** Executive Summary rewritten to open with the global treatment gap; India cited as an illustrative example. JU-01 renamed from "Urban Indian Professional (Primary)" to "Urban Professional". Domain Requirements section renamed from "India — Primary Market" to "Phase 1 Launch Market — India". Innovation Analysis competitive table retained as-is (India incumbents remain relevant to the launch market analysis).
**Rationale:** Product is designed for any therapist-scarce, high-growth market. India-first framing was limiting investor and partnership positioning.

### Decision 2: Cost objective removed from Executive Summary and Success Criteria
**Signal:** Product owner direction.
**Change:** Price point (₹200–500/month) removed from Executive Summary. "Positive unit economics at 12 months: LTV > 3× CAC at ₹200–500/month pricing" removed from Success Criteria. Section renamed from "India Business Objectives (Year 1)" to "Phase 1 Launch Objectives (Year 1)".
**Rationale:** Monetisation mechanics are deferred post-MVP; including a specific price target in the PRD creates a premature commitment.

### Decision 3: MVP split into MVP (Core Platform) + Phase 1 (India Launch)
**Signal:** Product owner direction.
**Change:** "MVP — India Launch" section split into two:
- **MVP — Core Platform:** ERP protocol, CBT/somatic techniques, hierarchy builder, ERP session flow, notifications, analytics, generic crisis detection (English, market-configured helpline numbers), i18n framework (English), security baseline.
- **Phase 1 — India Launch (new section):** DPDPA 2023 compliance, India national crisis helplines (updated to current numbers — see below), Hindi crisis keyword detection, mini-SPIN ≥6 clinical input requirement, Hindi i18n activation, Android-primary device profile validation, India SLA and load testing targets.
**Rationale:** Separating the market-agnostic platform from India-specific compliance and localisation makes the product portable to other markets without restructuring the PRD.
